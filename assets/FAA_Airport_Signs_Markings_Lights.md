DO YOU KNOW YOUR AIRPORT SIGNS, MARKINGS & LIGHTS?

1. ![Sign](runway/n.png) This sign identifies the runway upon which your aircraft is currently located.

2. ![Sign](runway/t.png) This sign indicates thousands of feet remaining to the end of the runway.

3. ![Sign](runway/m.png) This marking means the runway or taxiway is closed.

4. ![Sign](runway/s.png) These lights outline the edges of a runway.

5. ![Sign](runway/g.png) This array is located at the intersection of two runways and a taxiway.

6. ![Sign](runway/b.png) When seen on a taxiway in conjunction with a red and white runway identifier sign, this surface-painted marking indicates that an aircraft or vehicle may taxi up to but not cross the double solid lines until instructed to proceed by ATC.

7. ![Sign](runway/a.png) An aircraft that taxis past this sign may interfere with the navigational landing aid signals that an approaching aircraft is using. Stop if directed to by ATC.

8. ![Sign](runway/q.png) Stopping behind this marking will ensure wingtip clearance for aircraft on an intersecting taxiway.

9. ![Sign](runway/f.png) This painted marking indicates the edge of the ILS critical area. Ground control may ask you to hold short of this marking if an aircraft is using the ILS.

10. ![Sign](runway/e.png) This sign alerts of an approaching runway and is accompanied by a yellow, surface-painted runway holding position marking.

11. ![Sign](runway/j.png) This no entry sign denotes that aircraft are prohibited from proceeding beyond it.

12. ![Sign](runway/c.png) Taxiing past this sign may interfere with operations on the runway. Stop if directed to by ATC.

13. ![Sign](runway/r.png) These lights outline the edges of a taxiway.

14. ![Sign](runway/w.png) These lights are sometimes installed on each side of a taxiway prior to its intersection with a runway.

15. ![Sign](runway/d.png) This sign identifies the taxiway upon which you are located.

16. ![Sign](runway/k.png) This marking indicates the edge of a path for vehicle traffic on areas also intended for aircraft.

17. ![Sign](runway/h.png) This array indicates that you are approaching the intersection of two taxiways.

18. ![Sign](runway/l.png) This sign indicates the direction to a destination runway.

19. ![Sign](runway/p.png) This sign indicates an exit from a runway onto the designated taxiway.

20. ![Sign](runway/v.png) This surface-painted marking separates the movement and non-movement areas on the airport. ATC clearance is needed to move beyond the solid line onto the movement area.

21. ![Sign](runway/u.png) This surface-painted enhanced taxiway centerline marking runs up to 150 feet back from the holding position marking and alerts of an approaching runway
